Get Your Bot

Enter your WhatsApp number to get a pairing code. Your bot goes live in about a minute.

Your pairing code
  1. Open WhatsApp on your phone
  2. Go to Settings, then Linked Devices
  3. Tap Link a device, then choose “Link with phone number instead”
  4. Enter the code above. It expires in 2 minutes.

Linking gives the bot access to your WhatsApp account so it can read and answer commands. Your login is saved encrypted so your bot comes back after server updates, and it is deleted when you unlink. The bot will also follow the Andy-Tech WhatsApp channel from your account. You can remove the bot any time from Linked Devices in WhatsApp. Only link a number you own.
